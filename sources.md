## Short source-claim evidence candidates

These quotations identify inspected supporting passages; they are not independent review sign-offs. Each quotation is shorter than the source-claim limit.

- **longden2017**, Results, “Elevation of K+ around capillaries”: “we measured the SM cell membrane potential at Zone 2”. Depth: fulltext. Supports direct electrical measurement at the arteriole.
- **moshkforoush2020**, Methods, “Multicellular Models”: “Rgj has not been measured in capillaries”. Depth: fulltext. Supports explicit coupling uncertainty.
- **isaacs2024**, Discussion: “direct action of Ba2+ on pericyte Kir channels”. Depth: fulltext. Supports nonlocal pharmacological ambiguity; the surrounding paragraph also mentions other cells.
- **longden2026**, Future Directions: “injecting currents of varying magnitude and polarity into two downstream branches simultaneously”. Depth: fulltext. Supports the prior paired-electrode design.
- **kapela2018**, Discussion, “Endothelial Rgj”: “our estimation of EC-to-EC coupling resistance depends on the assumed geometry of ECs and the spatial arrangement”. Depth: fulltext; read by the scientific audit agent.
- **behringer2012**, Results, “Effects of SKCa/IKCa activation on electrical conduction over distance”: “At each distance, opening SKCa/IKCa with 1 μmol/L NS309 reduced CA yet the I–V relationship remained linear.” Depth: fulltext; chemical subscripts flattened for the evidence ledger, read by the scientific audit agent.
- **rivers1997**, Crossref abstract. Depth: abstract; paraphrase only, as full text was not inspected.
- **postnov2015**, “Regenerative Pulse Transmission and Whole-Cell Electric Properties”: “the presence of negative whole-cell resistance (or conductance)”. Depth: fulltext. Supports the prior dynamical mechanism.
- **hariharan2022**, Results, paragraph beginning “The glucose concentration in bulk cerebrospinal fluid,” and Fig. 4; quote: “pericyte resting Vm averaged −36 mV”. Depth: fulltext.

## Frozen versions and rights

The Crossref responses preserve registry metadata retrieved on the audit date. Full-text copies are research inspection materials; their respective publisher/PMC rights apply and no license to republish all article text is asserted. Longden2017 is a Nature author manuscript under the academic-use terms recorded in its XML. Isaacs2024 and Hariharan2022 specify CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; Postnov2015 specifies CC BY. Moshkforoush2020 specifies the PNAS license. Longden2026, Kapela2018, and Behringer2012 were inspected through PMC author-manuscript HTML, without inferring additional redistribution rights. No raw external experimental dataset was analyzed.

The official Moshkforoush code was inspected at https://github.com/ntsoukias/Capillary-Kir-Model, commit SHA `759d6a9f2002ab7367791c65118500091749d75e`; frozen `moshk_Fig4C.m` and `moshk_Fig3AB.m` implement the reported model. Its MIT license is preserved as `moshk_LICENSE.txt`. Our implementation is an independently written reduced network model, with discrepancies and assumptions recorded in the parameter inventory. Retrieved materials and hashes are enumerated in `simulation/data/source_evidence/manifest.json`.

`simulation/fetch_sources.py` lists exact primary URLs, including commit-pinned model-code links. It accesses the network only with `--refresh`; analysis does not download data. Failed SI response bodies have explicit failed-response names and are excluded from substantive evidence.


## Additional prior work and inspected support

All seven additions use their versions of record. The source filenames below distinguish registry identity from full-text inspection. Crane, Neild and Segal's all-capital registry names are normalized to ordinary capitalization for the bibliography; nonbreaking whitespace in names is normalized. A missing space between the potassium superscript and “Current” in the Wessel registry title was restored for typesetting. Harraz has article-number `e38689`; its CSL page field uses that locator.

| Citation ID | Depth and material inspected | Argument engaged and limit |
|--------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| wessel1999 | Fulltext: PMC6783099, Results “Modeling” and Fig. 7; Discussion; `wessel1999.fulltext.html` and `.txt` | Paired excitatory inputs in leech show Kir-dependent supralinearity, reproduced with Kir as the only voltage-dependent conductance. The models shown are one- and two-compartment models; the Discussion proposes a same-branch coincidence effect. This is prior Kir-mediated summation, with depolarization deactivating Kir, distinct from hyperpolarizing capillary recruitment. |
| rall1967 | Identity: Crossref, PubMed PMID 6055351 and publisher landing page | Historical precedent for distinguishing input distributions with cable responses. The publisher PDF returned HTTP 403 and PubMed supplies no abstract. No particular result, equation, page or section is represented as inspected, and no specific source claim is entered in the claim ledger. |
| crane2004 | Abstract: Crossref-registered abstract | Hamster cheek-pouch recordings found conducted hyperpolarization exceeding the passive prediction. This supplies experimental precedent for active membrane contributions in arterioles; it does not locate capillary Kir integration. No full text inspected. |
| diep2005 | Fulltext: PMC1474767, Results on cell orientation/coupling and Discussion; `diep2005.fulltext.html` and `.txt` | Resistance-artery models show that cellular arrangement and coupling determine longitudinal versus cross-layer spread, and that coupling transforms transient events into sustained voltage responses. These are skeletal-muscle arterial parameters. |
| hald2012 | Fulltext: PMC3309280, “Reaching steady state,” Discussion and Conclusion; `hald2012.fulltext.html` and `.txt` | A successful exponential fit to conduction can coexist with nonlinear membrane dynamics; fitted length constants depend on stimulus and observation time. An exponential spatial response alone does not establish passive propagation. |
| segal2015 | Fulltext: PMC4670584, endothelial-tube discussion preceding “Summary and translation”; `segal2015.fulltext.html` and `.txt` | Increasing membrane conductance can dissipate conducted signals while junctions remain patent. This review places the Behringer experiments in the broader resistance-network physiology; its setting is chiefly skeletal muscle. |
| harraz2018 | Fulltext: publisher XML, version 2, Results on reciprocal channel regulation and Fig. 6C; `harraz2018.fulltext.xml` and `.txt` | PGE2-mediated PIP2 depletion activates TRPV4 and suppresses Kir with reported fitted time constants of 4.9 and 6.4 minutes, respectively. These are room-temperature pharmacological measurements in a high-potassium bath with channel agonist/blocker conditions. Applying their timescale to a potassium-only network is an assumption; they do not establish a potassium-triggered reset law. |
